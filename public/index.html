HOW TO PLAY

Placing tiles

Tap a tile on your rack, then tap a square. Or tap an empty square and type: the cursor moves along as you go (tap the square again to switch between across and down). Backspace takes a tile back and Enter plays. Every word you make must be in the dictionary. British, Irish and American spellings all count.

DUEL scoring

Only your main word scores. Side-words you make along the way must still be real words, but they add nothing, so hooking and stacking tricks aren't worth much.

Your score is the letter values, plus a length bonus (4 letters +2, 5 +5, 6 +9, 7 +14, 8 +20, and more beyond), plus +5 for each shell you cover. Use 7 tiles at once for another +20. Shells are flat bonuses, so it doesn't matter which letter sits on them.

The tide: fall 20 or more points behind and the tide comes in for you. Your words score ×1.25 (×1.5 from 50 behind, ×1.75 from 90 behind), you hold an 8th tile, and you can swap up to 3 tiles for free before you play. The scoreboard shows when it's running.

The game ends when someone uses their last tile and the bag is empty, or after 6 scoreless turns in a row.

LIGHTHOUSE (co-op)

Lanterns are scattered across the board, each with a number. To light one, make a word at least that long that runs through it. A lantern square only accepts a tile that lights it, so you can't waste one, but you can box one in by building too closely around it. Light them all before the shared turns run out.

Both racks are visible to both of you, so plan together. Once a turn you can GIVE one tile to your partner before you play: tap the tile, then GIVE.
